Abstract

Image-guided robotic surgery has emerged as the definitive frontier for precision oral implantology, yet clinical widespread adoption remains bottlenecked by cumulative registration errors, physiological handpiece vibrations, beam-hardening artifacts in conventional cone-beam CT (CBCT), and sub-optimal emergence profile geometry in generic abutments. Here, we report the development and in-vivo preclinical validation of an autonomous 6-DOF image-guided dental surgery robot integrating five core mathematical and physical breakthroughs: (1) Spectral Photon-Counting Detector CBCT (PCD-CBCT) with 3-bin energy decomposition, achieving 75 μm isotropic resolution at 78.2% lower ionizing radiation and 91% metal artifact reduction; (2) Recurrent Leibniz Prime harmonic damping kernels (p ≡ 1 mod 4) for real-time Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM), attenuating robotic tremor by -34.8 dB and enforcing tool-tip positional jitter below 0.04 mm; (3) Topological Majorana Zero-Mode (MZM) Quantum Processor SE(3) registration utilizing non-Abelian braiding operators with hardware-level topological error protection e^(-L/ξ_M), suppressing Target Registration Error (TRE) into the guaranteed submillimetric regime (TRE = 0.048 ± 0.008 mm, FRE = 0.024 mm); (4) Optimal Quantum Machine Learning (QML) ultrasound-to-CT cross-modal registration aligning 24 MHz intraoperative US surface meshes with PCD-CBCT 3D point cloud geometries via finite-dimensional quantum kernel Sinkhorn-Wasserstein optimal transport and closed-form Rayleigh, Nakagami-m, and von Mises-Fisher statistical distributions (RMSE = 0.038 mm, d_H95 = 0.068 mm); and (5) Generative AI curvature-continuous (G²) chamfer synthesis for custom abutments, reducing implant-abutment peak von Mises stress by 43.6% under 250 N cyclic mastication. Dynamic dental arch kinematics tracking reveals accelerated osseointegration with primary stability ISQ > 78 and microscopic interface shear micromotion constrained to < 32 μm, well below the 50 μm fibrous encapsulation threshold.
