STEVE MANN'S MERSIVITY & VEILLANCE THEORY

Grounding water sensing in Humanistic Intelligence (HI), phenomenological augmented reality, and citizen-driven sousveillance.

👁️

Hydro-Sousveillance

Shifting water monitoring from closed municipal surveillance to open citizen-led sousveillance ("watching from below"). Open-access optical sensor arrays allow community members to inspect real-time water clarity without municipal opacity.

"Sousveillance empowers citizens to monitor the monitoring mechanisms, turning water quality into shared civic knowledge."
🌊

Mersive Computing

Water is both the environment and the computational medium. Mersivity merges bodily immersion with real-time bio-telemetry, enabling direct physical interaction with aquatic data streams along the Spadina Quay.

"In a mersive space, the boundary between human, water, and computer dissolves into continuous feedback."
👓

Phenomenological AR (PAR)

Visualizing imperceptible turbidity variations, E. coli gradients, and sediment plumes by superimposing real-time NTU field vectors directly onto physical water surfaces through smart optics and underwater camera arrays.

"Augmented reality becomes a tool of environmental truth when tuned to natural physical fields."

Humanistic Intelligence (HI)

Coupling human intuition with algorithmic NTU prediction models. Citizens and watershed managers operate as feedback loops in the control architecture, guiding bio-retention baffle throttling during storm surges.

"HI puts humans in the loop, creating cyborg ecosystems that adapt organically to climate stress."
